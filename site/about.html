About

What does a godly Nigerian statesman look like?

The Statesmen League was not built around a political party or a policy agenda. It was built around that question. It is harder to answer than it sounds, and answering it is the first work of formation.

The Ideal Statesman

We have spent time defining this carefully.

The Ideal Statesman is someone whose character precedes his competence: whose inner life, convictions and patterns of behaviour qualify him before his CV does. We draw from two archetypes in Scripture.

Daniel

Served faithfully in foreign government without surrendering his convictions. He was trusted by kings who did not share his faith, because his character was beyond question.

David

Led with courage, accountability and a heart turned toward God. Even when he failed, his orientation held.

These are not merely inspirational figures. They are templates. The Statesmen League is building people who can stand in Daniel's position and lead in David's manner.

The founding

The League was co-founded by Raphael Ojo-Williams and Joshua Paul-lasisi.

Raphael Ojo-Williams

Founding vision

Raphael carries the founding vision, rooted in the conviction that Christians must not abandon the public square, and that the governance mountain belongs to those who are being formed to occupy it faithfully.

Joshua Paul-lasisi

Operations

Joshua manages operations: building the systems, the curriculum structure and the chapter framework that make the vision workable at scale.

What we believe

Character precedes competence.

A nation gets the leadership its character produces. When a generation of leaders emerges that has been genuinely formed (not merely educated, not merely ambitious), the quality of governance changes.

Formation takes time, deliberate effort and community. We are committed to the long work, and we believe it is worth doing.

The Statesmen League

Forming young Nigerians in character and civic life for service in government and public life.
