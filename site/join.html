Join the League

The Statesmen League is not for everyone. It is for those who have looked at Nigeria's governance and decided that the answer is formation, not cynicism or passivity.

If you are a young Nigerian who takes your responsibility to this nation seriously, and you are willing to submit to a deliberate process of formation in character and civic life, we would like to hear from you.

Registration

The form takes about five minutes. We ask for some personal background and two short written responses. Take your time with the written section. It is the part we read most carefully.

Open the form full screen

After you apply, someone from the Founding Council will be in touch. We review applications as they come in. If you have questions before applying, you can reach us at thestatesmenleague@gmail.com.
